Alfred AI — Privacy Policy

Effective: today's date · Contact: cyberartificial1@gmail.com

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, and a salted, hashed password (we never store your password in readable form).
  • Chats: the messages you send and the answers Alfred generates.
  • Memories: facts you choose to save with /remember, so Alfred can personalize answers.
  • Usage: daily message counts per plan, and technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

2. How we use it

To provide your account and chat history, remember what you asked us to remember, enforce plan limits, keep the service secure, and improve quality. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party AI providers

Required disclosure: to generate answers, your messages are sent to external AI provider APIs operated by independent companies. Those providers process the content under their own privacy policies and infrastructure, which may be located in other countries. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat that you would not want processed for the purpose of generating an answer.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Chats: kept until you delete them. Deleting a chat in the app permanently removes its messages.
  • Memories and account deletion: email cyberartificial1@gmail.com from your account address and we will delete your saved memories or your entire account.

5. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Session tokens are stored hashed. Access to the operating systems running the service is restricted to the operator.

6. Age

Alfred is intended for users aged 16 and older. This is a product decision of the service.

7. Your choices

You can delete chats in the app at any time, decline to save memories, and request deletion of memories or your account by email as described above.

8. Changes

As the service evolves (for example, if payments are added), this policy will be updated and significant changes announced in the app.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: cyberartificial1@gmail.com
